I'm Antone King, a Generative AI Solution Architect, farmer, husband, and father of three in Middletown, Delaware.

## The work

At **ServiceNow**, I help design AI-powered workflows and agentic automation at enterprise scale. I'm interested in the distance between an idea that works in a demo and a system that works in the real world.

My personal experiments focus on computer vision and robotics. The farm gives me a place to ask concrete questions, work through the fundamentals, and learn from what happens outside the screen. [Pixel Lab]({{ '/projects/pixel-lab/' | relative_url }}) is one way I'm making that learning tangible.

The projects and writing here are my own personal work.

## The rest of life

I'm a Christian, a husband, and a father. The teachings of Jesus and Charlie Munger's mental models are two lenses I keep coming back to. I train for resilience, speed, and a clear head.

I live on a small farm, where I tend to sheep and goats. I also appreciate a good brisket smoke. These parts of life have a way of finding their way into how I think about systems, patience, and useful work.

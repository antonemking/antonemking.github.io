I'm exploring a small rover as a way to learn about cameras, data collection, and robot perception on a farm. The first question is a practical one: what would make a set of field images useful for later analysis?

## Questions I’m working through

- How do camera placement, motion, and changing light affect the images?
- What context needs to accompany each recording to make it useful later?
- How can a repeatable test distinguish a promising idea from an improvement that holds up?

The work sits alongside my study of computer vision and multi-view geometry. It's a place to connect the math to physical constraints and to be precise about what has actually been tested.

  <strong>Current status: prototype planning and bench work</strong>
  <p>This is an early hardware exploration. Autonomous operation and field performance have not been demonstrated. There are no field results to report yet.</p>
